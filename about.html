About ACK St. Faith’s Church

Rooted in Anglican heritage, growing in faith, and serving God’s people with love.

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurture believers through discipleship, and serve the community in love and truth.

Our Vision

A Christ-centered church transforming lives and communities for the glory of God.

Our Values

Faithfulness to Scripture, unity in fellowship, servant leadership, integrity, and compassion for all.

History of ACK St. Faith’s Church

ACK St. Faith’s Parish, Ongata Rongai, was established on 28th March 1982 following deliberations by a planning committee from St. Barnabas Church, Otiende.

The parish was officially named ACK St. Faith’s by the then Archbishop of Kenya, the Most Rev. Manasses Kuria, on 16th July 1982.

What began as a small fellowship meeting in homes has grown into a vibrant parish serving over 800 members and impacting the wider Ongata Rongai community.

The Anglican Church of Kenya

The Anglican Church of Kenya traces its roots to 1844 with the arrival of Dr. Ludwig Krapf at the Kenyan coast. Together with fellow missionaries, the Gospel was preached, Scriptures translated, and communities transformed.

Over the decades, the church expanded inland, establishing schools, theological institutions, and parishes across the country.

In 1998, the Church of the Province of East Africa was officially renamed the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK), affirming its identity and mission within the global Anglican Communion.

Key Milestones

1844

Dr. Ludwig Krapf arrives in Mombasa, marking the beginning of Anglican mission in Kenya.

1970

Election of Archbishop Festo Olang’ as the first African Archbishop.

1998

Church officially renamed the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK).
