About ACK St. Faith’s Church

Rooted in Anglican heritage, growing in faith, and serving God’s people with love.

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurture believers through discipleship, and serve the community in love and truth.

Our Vision

A Christ-centered church transforming lives and communities for the glory of God.

Our Values

Faithfulness to Scripture, unity in fellowship, servant leadership, integrity, and compassion for all.

History of St. Faith’s Parish (1983 - 2023)

Origins and Formation

The History of St. Faith’s Parish Ongata Rongai traces back to 28th March 1982 when a committee was formed at St. Barnabas Church Otiende. The proposed church was named ACK St. Faith’s by Archbishop Manasses Kuria on 16th July 1982. Meetings for planning the temporary church took place at Mr. & Mrs. Sospeter Gichuru’s house.

The parish grew from five families to the current 800 members.

First Church Building

Fundraising started on 4th July 1982, raising Ksh. 8,124.05. Construction began in August 1982 on a plot next to Fig Tree Academy. Official prayers began on 12th September 1982 under Rev. Joseph Leboo. The parish was inaugurated on 30th January 1983 with Rev. Manasseh Ngugi as first Vicar.

The first PCC included Rev. Ngugi, Mr. Johnson Mwangi, Mr. Sospeter Gichuru, and other members.

Moving to the New Site

Resolution passed in the AGM to move the Church to a new site. First service at the new site was held on 24th April 1983. First Holy Communion and baptism service was on 1st May 1983.

Permanent Church Building

Construction started in 1987, consecration of site by Most Rev. Manasses Kuria on 16th August 1987. Architect Mr. Peter Munene tabled plans. Church completed in October 1998 and consecrated on 17th October 1999.

Growth & Daughter Churches

St. Faith’s Parish gave rise to several daughter churches, many now independent parishes:

  • St. Peter’s Emakoko Parish – 24/7/1985
  • St. Paul’s Kandisi Parish – 11/2/1996
  • St. Mark’s Enkutoto Parish – 1997
  • St. Thomas Ongata Rongai Parish – 10/2/2002
  • St. Bartholomew’s Church – 15/9/2002
  • St. Michael’s & All Angels – 1/12/2002
  • St. Luke’s Najile Church – Adopted 2003
  • St. John’s the Apostle Parish Kware – 15/08/2010
  • St. Stephen’s Church – 15/07/2016
  • ACK South Sudan Service – 2015 (Dinka language)
