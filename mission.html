Mission Team

Sharing Christ's Love Through Action

Our Mission

As a dedicated team, the Mission Team follows Christ's footsteps (Luke 6:27-30) to reach the lost, needy, and vulnerable, sharing the Gospel (Matthew 28:19-20) and promoting community unity.

Eng. George Dawo

Director of Missions

Eng. George Dawo

Eng. Dawo has faithfully led the Mission Team for over 8 years, coordinating mission activities, overseeing outreach, and ensuring the love of Christ is shared practically and spiritually.
